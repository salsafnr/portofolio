## Problem

Paper-based motor inspection records made it impossible to track motor condition history or spot trends over time.

## Solution

Web applications with trackable records, trend-analysis charts, and automated report generation. A self-built WhatsApp alert bot (Node.js, Baileys) avoids paid messaging services.

## Scope

About 200 motors are registered, tracking vibration, envelope, temperature, and grease.
