## Problem

A false vibration reading can trigger an automatic shutdown, and restarting the plant is costly.

## Solution

I estimated each of the 4 vibration points from the other three, using their mechanical coupling. A validation logic cross-checks an over-limit sensor against its estimate and its neighboring sensors.

## Result

Implemented in the DCS, with approximately 6% error versus live readings in real-time spot checks.
